What to check before ordering

Match the variant and size

Confirm the seller, color and size on the live listing. Compare garment measurements with an item you already own. For shoes, check whether the chart refers to foot length or insole length.

Review warehouse QC photos

When available, check that warehouse photos show your exact variant. Look at the size label, seams and visible defects. Ask for measurements if the photos leave the fit unclear.

Estimate the delivered cost

Add the item price, domestic delivery, agent fees and international shipping. Check whether your route charges by actual or volumetric weight, and include any applicable duties or taxes.

PikoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the product links go?

Each product name opens the corresponding HipobuyFind listing. Both buttons at the top open the HipobuyFind homepage.

Can I paste a catalog link into PikoBuy?

Open the catalog item and locate its original seller link. Check whether PikoBuy accepts that URL and displays the same product and variant before placing an order.

What does the USD column include?

It shows the approximate USD equivalent of the listed item price. Delivery, agent fees and applicable taxes must be calculated separately.

Where can I see QC photos?

Check the listing and your agent’s warehouse tools for available photos. Match the photos to the ordered variant, especially if several colors or versions share one listing.
